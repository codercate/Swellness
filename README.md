# 🌊 Swellness

*Show up for the things that make you feel alive.*

Swellness is an ocean-inspired accountability app designed to help women build a consistent relationship with surfing and the ocean.

The idea came from a simple observation: most surf apps focus on the conditions. Swellness focuses on the person.

Surfing isn't just another workout to check off a list. It's something that helps people feel grounded, present, and more like themselves. But life gets busy, motivation comes and goes, and it's easy to lose momentum.

Swellness exists to help people keep showing up.

Commit to your session.
Close the app.
Go surf.
Come back and reflect.

No pressure.
No performance tracking.
Just momentum.

## ✨ Core Features

### 🏄 Surf Accountability

Build consistency by making small commitments to yourself.

- Set surf intentions
- Track your streaks
- Celebrate showing up, not perfection

### 🌊 Session Reflection

Capture what each surf session meant to you.

- Journal after each session
- Reflect on your mood and confidence
- Notice patterns over time

### 📈 Your Surf Story

Rather than measuring success by wave count or performance, Swellness helps you build your identity as someone who makes time for the things that make them feel alive.

### 🔮 Ocean Oracle *(Planned Feature)*

Ocean Oracle is a personalized surf recommendation feature that helps answer one simple question:

> "Where should I surf today?"

Instead of only showing surf forecasts, Ocean Oracle combines ocean conditions with local surf knowledge to recommend spots based on your experience level, preferred board, goals, and the kind of session you're looking for.

Long term, Ocean Oracle may grow into its own standalone app, but it begins as part of the larger Swellness experience.

## 🎯 Vision

My goal isn't to build another surf forecasting app.

It's to create a product that helps people build a lifelong relationship with the ocean.

If checking Swellness helps someone choose the ocean over scrolling, paddle out one more time this week, or remember why they fell in love with surfing in the first place, then it's doing exactly what it was designed to do.
